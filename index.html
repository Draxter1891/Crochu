From a hobby to a happy little shop

My grandmother taught me to crochet at her kitchen table when I was nine, and I never really put the hook down. What began as gifts for friends slowly grew into Loop & Bloom — a one-woman studio where every blanket, plushie, and beanie is made slowly and with intention.

I choose soft, ethically-sourced yarns, crochet everything to order (no dusty warehouse stock here!), and wrap each parcel by hand with tissue, twine, and a little note just for you.

When I'm not crocheting, you'll find me sipping lavender tea, rearranging my yarn wall for the hundredth time, or being "supervised" by my cat, Miso.

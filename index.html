Behind the Crochets

Hi, I'm Charu Sharma — the hands behind the crochets

Crochet started with one ball of blush-pink yarn and a very patient cat.

2 yrs

of happy hooking

From a hobby to a happy little shop, every stitch in our story began at home.

I first discovered the art of crochet through my grandmother and my bua, who patiently taught me the beauty of turning a simple thread into something meaningful. What started as a childhood hobby slowly became a passion—a quiet little corner of creativity that I kept returning to.

With time, I began experimenting, creating pieces for myself, and gifting them to family and friends. Their love and encouragement made me realize that this handmade art could be shared with many more people.

Today, this passion has grown into a small business, but the heart remains the same: creating every piece with patience, care, and the warmth of something made by hand - just as I learned at home.
